Phase 6: Discounts and stock count

Three small pieces. Each follows its own business switch: Line discount, Sale discount, Stock count (all on by default until the backend sends them). Look at the screens, read "My calls" at the bottom, and tell me what to change.

Long-press a cart line, or tap its new "%" button, to give that line a discount: a percent or an amount. The old price stays visible, struck through. Receipt, history and reports use the discounted price.

3. Stock count (switch: stock_count_enabled)

Opens from Inventory ("Stock count"). Scan or search a product to add it, type what is on the shelf; the app shows the difference. Applying turns each difference into one stock adjustment (reason "Stock count"), all together or none. Works offline.

My calls (correct me)

  • Who can discount. Owner and manager apply discounts directly. A cashier does not see the Discount links. (Desktop asks a cashier for the owner's approval; the phone has no such pop-up, so I hide it.)
  • Percent or amount. Both, on one sheet. Amount can never exceed the line (or the sale). Percent 0.01 to 100. Saved as an exact amount, so receipt and history agree to the cent.
  • Order of maths. Line discount first, then the sale discount on the sum of discounted lines, then tax on what is left (this is what the phone's sale maths and the server already do).
  • Switch off. The Discount links and the "Add discount" row disappear. A cart that already has a discount keeps it until charged (a till switched off mid-sale does not lose the customer's price).
  • Changing the cart. A percent discount follows quantity changes (10% of the new line). An amount discount is cut to the line if you reduce the quantity. A sale discount given as percent follows the total; as amount it is cut to the total.
  • Stock count: how it is stored. Not with the server's stock.count (manager only, absolute numbers, breaks offline). Each difference is a signed stock.adjust, like desktop. So the switch is a till rule only; the server does not enforce it for count.
  • Difference = counted minus the stock shown when that row was first counted, so a sale made while you count does not spoil it. Negative counts refused. Only active products that track stock.
  • Who can count. Anyone signed in, in the shop they work in (the server allows stock adjust for cashiers). No owner approval pop-up on the phone.
  • No draft. Leaving a count in progress asks "Throw away the count?" (same as desktop).
  • Not in this phase: price override, discount on custom amounts' reasons, discount in sale history filters.
One question: should a cashier be allowed to give discounts at all (with a PIN of the owner), or is "owner and manager only" right for now?
