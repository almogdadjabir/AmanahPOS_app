4. Rules I will build to

TopicBehaviour
Switchesshifts_mode off/auto/manual and cash_drawer_enabled from the dashboard (Phase 2 service). Off hides the chip, the More row and all shift screens.
Who can do whatOwner and manager always. Cashiers open/close/count only when shifts_cashiers_may_operate is on. Pay in and pay out: any signed-in user (same as desktop).
Sales and shiftsA sale is never blocked or delayed by a shift. Cash sales, refunds and debt payments are counted into the shift whose time range contains them (same as desktop).
Auto modeOpens and closes by schedule in business time, using the desktop's window maths. On mobile it runs only while the app is open (and on app start), so a shift may open or close late when the phone was closed. Closed shifts without a count show "Needs count".
OfflineEverything works offline and syncs later as shift.* and cash_movement.create. A rejected push shows in the existing sync conflicts list.
Several tillsOne open shift per till. Two tills in one shop only see each other's shifts once the backend sends shifts in pull (BE gap G5). Until then each phone shows its own.
Receipt / reportsNot changed in this phase.

5. Your call

1. Drawer off but shifts on. I propose the shift is just a work period with a sales summary (second Open-shift mockup). Opening cash, counting and pay in/out all need the drawer switch. Desktop today always counts cash; with the new switch it would become optional too. OK?
2. Auto shifts on a phone. Because the app cannot run a clock when closed, I propose the auto schedule is applied on app start, on return to foreground and every 30 s while open (like desktop). Late opens are stamped with the scheduled time, as desktop does. OK?
3. Where to put the chip. Next to the location chip in the POS top bar (shown above), or only inside More > Shifts and a badge? I recommend the top bar chip.
Needs the backend first (G1/G2): the cash_drawer_enabled field and a running shift.* / cash_movement.create. I will build against the sync contract and test with fakes, but cannot prove it against the real server until it is up.
