My calls (change any)

  • Who: owner and manager (same as the dashboard page). Cashiers get no entry.
  • Two ways in: Settings row, then Scan QR or Show code. Both lead to the same code screen. I do not make the normal product scanner react to the QR (it stays for barcodes only), and I do not add system-camera app links (needs files on app.amanapos.com, BE G7).
  • What a QR must be: any address that has link-device in its path. I do not read anything else from it. If the desktop QR looks different, tell me and I change one line.
  • Code life: 5 minutes counted from the server's time, bar and countdown. New code replaces the old one at once. Screen stays awake while the code shows.
  • Checking: the app asks the server every 3 seconds, stops when you leave or when the status is used, expired or replaced.
  • Business: the business you are signed in to. No picker on the phone.
  • Online only: nothing is saved on the phone, nothing goes to the outbox.
  • Not in this phase: device list or revoke (dashboard does it), choosing merge or new on the phone (desktop does it).
